# References

eTech's local competitors, captured 12 Oct 2026 at 1440px, full page.

## 00-avoid-*: what eTech must NOT look like

Five Malaysian PC shops and the ROG brand site. Between them they are the category
default Shop Receipt refuses: a full-width hero banner with a rig photo, carousels,
dark gaming or neon styling, icon-circle "why trust us" rows, stat counters
(10,000+ PCs built), review sliders and partner-logo strips.

If a page of ours could be swapped into this set without standing out, it fails.

- `00-avoid-dark-showroom-juspc.png`: black + red, poster-style best sellers
- `00-avoid-hero-and-trust-idealtech.png`: hero, "trusted by" logos, stats, icon grid
- `00-avoid-green-hero-mrit.png`: banner hero, icon circles, "save" tags
- `00-avoid-dark-distributor-suncycle.png`: dark news/distributor layout
- `00-avoid-category-bands-viewnet.png`: pastel full-width section bands
- `00-avoid-brand-hero-rog.png`: brand hero, condensed caps, dark cards

## 01+: parts worth borrowing

- `01-sku-price-list-suncycle.png`: Suncycle's Top Picks column. Category label,
  name, the maker's **SKU code** on its own line, and the **price aligned right**.
  Same idea as our catalogue code and dot-leadered price; theirs is proof that
  the local market reads a list like this. Borrow the structure, not the dark
  cards or the blue.
