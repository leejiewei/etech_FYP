Why we build on ASUS

Every PC starts with the motherboard: it decides which processor, memory, graphics card and drives the machine can take. Most of the PCs we assemble ourselves sit on an ASUS board, and the gaming ones add ASUS graphics cards, power supplies, liquid coolers and cases on top.

A PC is listed here when ASUS made it, or when its motherboard is ASUS. Each card says which other parts are ASUS too, read from that PC's own parts list, so the page changes when a build does.
