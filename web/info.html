About eTech

eTech Computer Accessories is a student-project PC shop offering laptops, monitors, gaming gear, networking equipment and PC components, plus a custom PC builder with compatibility checking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver nationwide? +
You choose at checkout: collect from the shop, or have it delivered to an address you give us. Collection is free and usually faster — we message you the moment it is ready.
Can I return a product? +
Yes, unused items in original packaging can be returned within 7 days.
Is checkout on this site real? +
This is a student FYP demo. There is no card payment and no gateway: the checkout shows bank details and asks for a photograph of the transfer receipt, which a member of staff checks by hand. During the test period the bank details are placeholders, so there is nothing to pay and no order is really fulfilled.
What is the difference between the two? +
If you collect, we put the order aside and message the phone number you gave at checkout when it is waiting at the counter — no address is asked for or kept. If you choose delivery, we ask for an address and send it there once your payment has been checked. Either way the phone number is how we keep you posted.
How does the PC Builder compatibility check work? +
It checks CPU/motherboard socket, RAM type (DDR4/DDR5), and whether your PSU wattage covers your GPU's needs.
