1. Who we are

eTech Computer Accessories sells computer parts, pre-built PCs, laptops, peripherals and accessories, and offers PC building and repair services. This website and app are operated by a student at a Malaysian university as part of a final-year project, under the supervision of an academic supervisor. The operator is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

2. Personal data we collect

TypeWhat it includes
Account details Your name, email address and phone number, and optionally a delivery address saved to your profile. Your password is held by our authentication provider as a hash — we never see or store the password itself.
Shopper profile Whether you shop as a Student, Gamer or Enterprise customer, chosen at sign-up and changeable on your profile page.
Orders The items you buy (including any colour you pick), the total, the name and phone number given at checkout, whether you chose collection or delivery, and a delivery address only if you chose delivery.

Rig showcase Photos of a PC you have built, the parts you tag, and your first name — only if you choose to submit a build.
Device information A random device identifier used when you browse without signing in, and, in the app, a notification token for your phone.

Browsing without signing in

If you are not signed in, your browser or device is given a random identifier (a UUID). It is not linked to your name, email or any other detail — it only tells us that several visits came from the same browser. If you later sign in or create an account, the activity recorded under that identifier on this device is attached to your account, so that a purchase can be read together with the browsing that led to it. Clearing your browser's site data starts a new identifier.

3. How we use your data

  • To run your account and orders — keeping your cart and wishlist, processing orders, arranging collection or delivery, and showing your order history.
  • To confirm payments — staff check uploaded receipts against each order.
  • To keep you updated — telling you when payment is approved or rejected, and when an order is ready or has been sent.
  • To personalise your shopping — the “Picked for you” section on the home page is built from your shopper profile, the products you view, wishlist and buy, and what customers with similar interests bought. It is shown only to signed-in customers.
  • To tell you about relevant offers — see section 4.
  • To answer your questions — through the AI assistant, WhatsApp, or the contact form.
  • To improve the shop — showing staff which products customers are interested in, and planning stock.
  • For the final-year project — shopping activity is the dataset studied in the project's dissertation, which asks whether a shop's own records can recommend products and forecast stock. It appears there only as totals, with no one named.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Notifications and offers

If you have an account, we send short messages that appear under the bell on the website and in the app, and on your phone if you use the app and allow notifications. There are three kinds:

  • Order updates about your own orders.
  • Deal alerts — when a product goes on a timed deal, we tell signed-in customers who saved it to their wishlist, left it in their cart, or viewed it three or more times. Staff choose the product; who is told is worked out automatically from shopping activity.
  • Weekly picks — at most one message a week, on Saturday morning, suggesting a product you have been looking at or one picked for you. The same product is not suggested to you twice within 30 days.

For each message we record when it was sent and whether you opened it, and for offers and picks we look at whether you viewed or bought that product in the following days. This tells us whether the messages are useful, and is reported only as totals.

Opting out: you can turn off phone notifications in your phone's settings at any time (messages will still wait under the bell). To stop deal alerts and weekly picks altogether, tell us through the Contact page.

5. Payments and receipts

We do not take card payments and there is no payment gateway. Checkout shows bank transfer details and asks you to upload a photo of the transfer receipt for staff to check. A receipt usually shows a name, an account or reference number and an amount, so please treat it like any bank document.

  • Receipts are stored privately and can be seen only by you and shop staff.
  • They are not used in the project's research or dissertation.
  • You can ask for yours to be deleted at any time.
  • During the test period there is nothing to pay, so please do not upload a real bank document.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers that run the shop for us, and only what each one needs:

ProviderPurpose
Supabase Database, sign-in and file storage, where all the data above is kept.
Vercel Hosts the website.
Google (Gemini API) Generates the AI assistant's replies. Your message and our product catalogue are sent; your name, email and account details are not.
Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) Delivers notifications to the app on your phone.
Meta (WhatsApp) Only if we message you about an order, or you message us.

WhatsApp: staff may use the phone number from your order to message you on WhatsApp about that order, for example to confirm payment or ask for a clearer receipt. Messages are written by a person, never sent automatically. Conversations are handled under WhatsApp's privacy policy and stay on their service after the order is finished. If you prefer, ask us to use email instead; you never need WhatsApp to buy anything.

Rig showcase: if you submit a build, its photos and your first name are published on the Rigs page once staff approve it. You can ask for it to be removed at any time.

We may also disclose data where the law requires us to.

7. Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, and there are no third-party analytics trackers on the site. Your browser's local storage (or the app's own storage) keeps your sign-in session, the device identifier described in section 2, and small settings such as whether you have dismissed the privacy banner.

8. How we protect your data

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules mean you can read only your own account and orders; shopping activity and receipts can be read only by shop staff. Passwords are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep your data safe.

9. How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept for as long as your account exists. Shopping activity, search and chatbot records are kept for the length of the project and its assessment, after which the project database is deleted.

10. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct it if it is wrong or out of date (most of it you can edit yourself on your profile page);
  • Withdraw consent and ask us to delete it — deleting your account removes your personal details, and any shopping records left behind are detached from you and cannot be traced back;
  • Limit marketing by opting out of deal alerts and weekly picks.

Taking part is voluntary: you can browse without an account and stop at any time. To clear the device identifier, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the shop changes what it collects or how it uses it. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact us

For any question about this policy, or to make a request about your data, please use our Contact page.
